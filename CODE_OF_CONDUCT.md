# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in
  a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or
contacting the maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and
result in a response deemed appropriate to the circumstances, which may
include a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban from the project's
spaces.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when an individual is representing the project in public
spaces.
